Zoom time: mouse wheel over the raster (or over the time axis). On a trackpad: two-finger vertical scroll, or pinch.
Zoom neurons: Shift + wheel, or wheel over the neuron axis on the left.
Pan: drag. Arrow keys also pan; so does a sideways two-finger swipe.
Box zoom: Ctrl + drag, ⌘ + drag on a Mac, or right-drag.
Jump: click or drag in the overview strip at the top.
Zoom to a probe or shank: click its label (or its color band) on the left.
Readout: click a cell to show its values (until the view changes; Esc clears), or hold Alt (Option on a Mac) to have it follow the pointer.
Keys: R reset, A all neurons, S whole session, + / − zoom time.
Raw voltage (only where the raw data are served, marked in yellow under the overview strip): with neurons plotted by depth and a window of 0.5 s or less, the recorded voltage is drawn under the spikes. V switches between the stored versions (lossless and lossy), D shows the difference from lossless, [ and ] set the gray range, O the opacity of the spikes, U hides it.

Each displayed cell is the mean firing rate over every neuron and instant it covers. Neurons sets the vertical axis: sorted by depth (probe, shank, then position along the shank, shallow at top, one row per neuron); plotted by depth (each shank to scale, each neuron a 15 µm band at its position, so neurons at similar depths overlap and gaps show as zero rate); or Rastermap order (similar neurons adjacent). In the first two, cells never average across a shank boundary: a cell that straddles one uses only the neurons of the shank under its center. In Rastermap order, cells average across probes. Spikes: all spikes, only tonic ones, only burst spikes, only burst onsets (the first spike of each burst, so the rate is bursts/s), or burst and tonic overlaid in magenta and green (both together add up to white). A burst is a run of at least K spikes of one unit with every ISI at most M ms, after at least S ms without a spike from that unit. The default (100 ms, 4 ms, 2 spikes) is the usual criterion for thalamic low-threshold bursts.
